Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Native, not printed Tracks, audio, MIDI, fades, automation, stretch, pitch, reverse, warp markers and Melodyne edits come across natively, both ways. Nothing is printed to audio.
  • Built-in effects Each DAW's built-in effects become the other's closest built-in effects. EQ, volume, pan and width match; compressors, limiters, reverbs and the like are close, not identical.
  • Plug-ins keep settings Third-party plug-ins keep their settings; they must be installed where you open the converted project.
  • First visit ~50 MB The first visit downloads about 50 MB (Python and ffmpeg for the browser); after that they are cached. Large projects need a computer with enough memory (roughly three times the project's size).
