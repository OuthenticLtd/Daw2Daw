Good to know

  
    
    
  • Native, not printedCubase → REAPER and REAPER → Cubase: tracks, audio, MIDI, fades, automation, stretch, pitch, reverse,
        warp markers and Melodyne edits come across natively; nothing is printed to audio.
    • 
    
  • Built-in effectsEach DAW's built-in effects become the other's closest built-in effects. EQ, volume, pan and width match;
        compressors, limiters, reverbs and the like are close, not identical.
    • 
    
  • Third-party plug-insThird-party plug-ins keep their settings; they must be installed where you open the converted project.
    • 
    
  • First visitThe first visit downloads about 50 MB (Python and ffmpeg for the browser); after that they are cached.
        Large projects need a computer with enough memory (roughly three times the project's size).
    • 
  

  
Uses Pyodide and ffmpeg.wasm (ffmpeg is GPL/LGPL).
